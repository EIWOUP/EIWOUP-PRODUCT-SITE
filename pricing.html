Questions

Before you pay.

What exactly do I get for free?

Every mode, every indicator, the full interface — running on EIMC’s demo (synthetic) data generator. Bar replay works on it too. It needs only a free account: no card and no expiry. What the free tier does not have is the live market. It exists so you can judge the product properly before paying for anything.

Do I need a broker or Dhan account?

No. Pro includes the live market data: EIMC streams it to the terminal, so there is no broker account to open, no data subscription to buy and no token to paste each morning. EIMC is an analysis tool and places no orders; trade with whichever broker you like.

What happens when the 1-day Pro trial ends?

The trial grants everything and is limited by time rather than by features. When it ends you are not locked out of the app — you drop back to the free tier and can keep using the whole interface on demo data, or pick a plan. Nothing you saved is deleted; your charts, drawings and recordings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EIMC.

Is this good for NIFTY 50 specifically?

That is what it was built around. An index has no order book and no volume, so most depth tools simply refuse to draw on NIFTY. EIMC records the near-month future’s 200-level book and shifts it onto the index chart by the basis, and its levels engine derives every line from swings, sweeps and session boundaries rather than from volume — so it works identically on NIFTY 50 and SENSEX. The DQRS setup rules were chosen for intraday index trading, and the volume profile was verified against a Dhan TradingView chart of NIFTY 50.

Why is Pro ₹299 and not ₹499?

₹299 a month is a launch price for our first customers. The regular price of Pro is ₹499 a month. Either way Pro includes every feature — there are no higher tiers to upgrade to.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your account. Pro stays on until the end of the month you paid for, then the terminal goes back to the Free plan on demo data. Your settings and drawings stay.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

By card or UPI Autopay through Razorpay, renewing each month. EIMC never sees your card number or UPI PIN. The terminal keeps its sign-in in the Windows Credential Manager, and its local server refuses any request whose Host or Origin header is not its own.

Is this investment advice?

No. EIMC is an analysis tool. It measures what has happened and shows what is resting in the book; the DQRS hit rates it reports are measured on the symbol and timeframe in front of you and are evidence, not forecasts. Every trading decision is yours.
