Licensing

How your licence behaves.

OFFLINE

It survives a dropped connection

Your licence is valid for 7 days and refreshes on every launch that can reach the server. If the network is down, EIMC keeps running on the cached licence until it genuinely expires. One failed DNS lookup must never lock a paying customer out of their charts during market hours.

SERVER

The app cannot grant itself features

Entitlement is decided server-side and signed into the token; the app only reads back what was verified. Nothing in the interface is the thing that unlocks a feature — otherwise anyone who can open DevTools would have the Elite tier.

DEVICE

One licence, your machine

A licence is bound to the device that activated it, so copying the licence file to another PC will not work. Switching machines is a sign-in, not a purchase.

Questions

Before you pay.

What exactly do I get for free?

Every mode, every indicator, the full interface — running on EIMC’s demo (synthetic) data generator. Bar replay works on it too. There is no account, no card and no expiry. What you cannot do on the free tier is connect your own Dhan account, so you are not looking at the live market. It exists so you can judge the product properly before paying for anything.

Do I need a Dhan account?

For any paid plan, yes — EIMC is a terminal for your Dhan account, not a data vendor. You need the Data APIs subscription for chart data. For Elite’s live orders you also need the Trading APIs enabled, and Dhan may require your IP to be whitelisted. Access tokens expire every 24 hours, so you paste a fresh one each day.

What happens when the 3-day Pro trial ends?

The trial grants everything and is limited by time rather than by features. When it ends you are not locked out of the app — you drop back to the free tier and can keep using the whole interface on demo data, or pick a plan. Nothing you saved is deleted; your charts, drawings and recordings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EIMC.

Why is the liquidity heatmap only on Elite?

Because it is the one feature with a real running cost on both sides. Dhan keeps no order-book history, so EIMC records its own — once a second, 09:00 to 15:35 IST, whether the app is open or not — and it holds four of your account’s five 200-level depth sockets to do it. Elite also carries live real-money order routing, which is the part that has to be right every single time.

Is this good for NIFTY 50 specifically?

That is what it was built around. An index has no order book and no volume, so most depth tools simply refuse to draw on NIFTY. EIMC records the near-month future’s 200-level book and shifts it onto the index chart by the basis, and its levels engine derives every line from swings, sweeps and session boundaries rather than from volume — so it works identically on NIFTY 50 and SENSEX. The DQRS setup rules were chosen for intraday index trading, and the volume profile was verified against a Dhan TradingView chart of NIFTY 50.

Monthly or yearly?

Yearly is billed as ten months, so two are free — Basic ₹4,990, Pro ₹14,990 and Elite ₹29,990 a year. If you are not sure the product fits your routine yet, start monthly or take the three-day Pro trial; the yearly saving will still be there next month.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. The tiers are additive — Pro is Basic plus the analysis engine, Elite is Pro plus recorded liquidity and live orders — so moving up only adds entitlements to your token and takes effect on the next launch.

Is my Dhan token safe?

Credentials go into the Windows Credential Manager through keyring, not into a config file in the install folder. The EIMC server is local-only: it refuses any request arriving with a Host or Origin header other than its own, so nothing on the network can talk to it.

Is this investment advice?

No. EIMC is an analysis tool. It measures what has happened and shows what is resting in the book; the DQRS hit rates it reports are measured on the symbol and timeframe in front of you and are evidence, not forecasts. Every trading decision is yours.
