Watch it work · nothing below is a mock-up

Real screens. Real sessions.

Five recordings and five screenshots taken straight from the terminal on live NIFTY data. Every frame is shown pixel for pixel, exactly as it looks in the app. The labels under each one are numbers read off that screen, so you can check every one against the picture.

REC 01 Order book · 200 levels

See the size waiting at every price, before price gets there.

This is the Book tab on NIFTY NOV FUT, recorded in market hours. The near-month future’s 200-level book is painted behind the candles as a heatmap. Every bright band is real size that sat at that price. On the right, the same book stands as a ladder that is re-read every time it updates.

  • 01
    Book read: the path of least resistanceUp to seven real measurements vote: resting orders, the whole exchange book, aggressive trades over five minutes, the size it takes to move price ten steps each way, thin book behind walls, price against VWAP and session volume nearby. The verdict says which side has the easier path, and how many inputs agree. In this clip it says No clear edge right now, and it is allowed to.
  • 02
    Who is in control, as one bar eachResting bids against asks, the exchange’s total buy and sell quantity, and who is hitting the market over one and five minutes. Here 55.6K bids rest against 82.5K asks, while aggressive sellers outpace buyers 65 / 35 over the last minute.
  • 03
    Walls that are measured, not guessedA price only counts as a wall at four times the typical size and in the top 5% of the book. The clip shows the bar it set: ≥ 1.36K, 130 typical. When the book behind a wall is thin, the panel tells you a break there can run fast.
  • 04
    The whole book, at any zoomShow ±10 to ±200 levels and group them 1× to 25×, so all 200 levels fit one screen. You get orders per level, the volume traded at each price and a depth curve of cumulative size on each side.
How it helps you

Your stop goes behind real size, not in front of it. Your target stops short of the next wall, not at a round number. Before a breakout, you can see whether there is anything behind the wall to slow it down. It is a rule-based reading of real orders, trades and volume. The app labels it, in its own words, “not a forecast”.

REC 02 Liquidity heatmap · Bookmap-style

Where the size sat, minute by minute, all session.

EIMC records the near-month future’s 200-level book every second and paints it behind the candles. Every horizontal band is size that rested at that price, for as long as it rested there. This clip runs from 10:50 to 12:40 on NIFTY NOV FUT, with the recorder on since 10:37.

  • 01
    Colour is sizeThin blue is ordinary resting size. Orange and red are the heavy orders. A band that runs for an hour is a level someone defended for an hour.
  • 02
    Walls appear, and walls get pulledA band that ends just before price reaches it was pulled, not filled. A band that price stops at, again and again, was real. You see both, because the book is recorded, not drawn afterwards.
  • 03
    The trades, over the bookThe candles, the trade bubbles and the buy and sell volume bars sit on top of the heatmap, so you can see price meet the size, and what happened next.
  • 04
    On the index, not just the futureNIFTY has no order book of its own. EIMC records the near-month future’s book and shifts it by the basis, so the bands line up with the index chart you trade from.
How it helps you

Support and resistance stop being lines you drew and become size you can see. You can tell a level that is being defended from one that is only a round number, and notice when the size behind a level quietly disappears.

REC 03 Option flow · who is strong today

Who is really in control, read from the options.

Option premiums carry the market’s real conviction, but nobody can read twelve of them at once in the middle of a trade. EIMC watches the ATM strike and one strike either side, three calls and three puts, and scores the four participants from 0 to 100: call buyers, call sellers, put buyers and put sellers.

  • 01
    A verdict in plain wordsPut sellers lead (100) — fresh writing (short buildup). It names the strongest participant, says how strong they are and why. You don’t have to decode a colour.
  • 02
    Bull camp against bear campCall buyers plus put sellers, against put buyers plus call sellers. In the clip it is 94 to 6, net +89, and the header reads STRONG BULLISH.
  • 03
    Calls and puts, side by sideEach side shows its buyer and seller scores over its own live chart. That lets you see writers absorbing a rally instead of guessing at it.
  • 04
    The session behind the numberSwitch between Strength, Premium and OI to see how the day built up. Here calls are +74.4% on premium while puts are −46.8%. A straight line means one side has been in control all session.
  • 05
    Any expiry, every 30 secondsFlip between expiries (17 Nov · 23 Nov · 01 Dec · 08 Dec in the clip) and the card scores them again. It refreshes every 30 seconds while the market is open.
How it helps you

It answers the one question a candle can’t: is this move being bought, or is it just not being sold? When put writers are pressing and calls are bid, dips tend to get absorbed. When the camps flip, the card flips with them, in plain words instead of a wall of open-interest numbers.

REC 04 Footprint, delta & volume profile

Inside every candle: who traded, at which price.

Zoom in and each candle opens into a footprint: the volume traded at every price inside it, with the delta, buyers minus sellers, printed under the candle. The recorded book stays behind it, and the profile on the price scale shows where the volume built up.

  • 01
    Volume at every priceEach row of a candle is one price with the size that traded there. Highlighted cells mark where one side clearly outweighed the other.
  • 02
    Delta under every candleBuyers minus sellers for that minute. In the clip a breakout candle prints +558, and the sharp drop after it prints −1,328: the move down was sold into, not drifted into.
  • 03
    The profile on the price scaleThe volume profile along the right edge shows which prices took the most trade, so the levels the market cared about stand out without drawing anything.
  • 04
    Candles, footprint and book togetherThe same chart carries the 200-level heatmap behind it and the buy and sell volume bars under it, so every candle can be read against the size that was waiting.
How it helps you

A green candle with negative delta is a warning a normal chart never gives you. The footprint shows whether a breakout was bought, whether a drop was real selling, and where the volume says the fair price is, one minute at a time.

SHOT 05 Technical bias · Overview tab

Twelve checks, one honest needle.

Every trader runs the same checklist in their head before an entry: trend, structure, momentum, VWAP, the higher timeframe. Under pressure, one of them gets skipped. The Technical bias card runs the whole checklist on every update and shows its working.

  • 01
    A gauge that says how sure it isStrong Bullish: uptrend, conviction 64%, trending. The needle sits between bear and bull, with the score beside it: +9 of a possible 14.
  • 02
    Every input listed, with its valueEMA 9/21, price against EMA 50, the EMA 50/200 cross, market structure, Supertrend, MACD, RSI 14, swing sequence, VWAP, DMI, volume and the higher-timeframe structure. Each one has a green, red or grey dot and the number behind it.
  • 03
    Disagreement stays visibleIn this read MACD disagrees (hist −1.86) and RSI sits neutral at 52.9. Nothing is averaged away, so you see exactly which inputs are against the call.
  • 04
    Weighted the way a trader wouldMarket structure and the next higher timeframe count double, which is why twelve checks add up to fourteen points. DMI only counts when ADX shows a real trend, and volume has to confirm.
How it helps you

It stops two expensive habits: trading against a trend you never checked, and talking yourself into a setup the indicators don’t support. Take one look before you enter. If the needle and your idea disagree, you find out before the order goes in.

SHOT 06 Golden volume profile · two screens

Every session’s volume, carried into the next.

The golden volume profile builds each session’s profile, TradingView-exact, with its POC, high-volume nodes and low-volume nodes, and carries it into the next day. Two screenshots from the morning of 19 Nov: a week of sessions at once, then yesterday’s profile laid against today’s price.

  • 01
    One profile for every sessionScreen 1 shows NIFTY on 1-minute candles from 11 to 18 Nov, each day with its own profile, its POC as an orange line and its HVN and LVN zones labelled. The bar at the bottom steps through them: Wed 18 Nov · +7 earlier → Thu 19 Nov.
  • 02
    Yesterday’s levels, already on today’s chartScreen 2 draws 18 Nov’s profile into 19 Nov: its POC and its nodes run across the new session while today’s own profile builds beside it. The levels are there at 9:15, not an hour into the session.
  • 03
    HVN: where the market agreedHigh-volume nodes are prices the market accepted. They behave like floors and magnets, and price tends to slow down or turn there.
  • 04
    LVN: where price travels fastLow-volume nodes are prices the market rejected. Price usually crosses them quickly, which makes them clean targets and poor places to wait for a fill.
How it helps you

A plan made before the open is a plan you can actually follow. Instead of reacting to every candle, you already know where price is likely to stall, where it is likely to run, and the level that proves your idea wrong.

The whole chain on one screen, already read for you.

Calls on the left, puts on the right, the strike in the middle, refreshed every five seconds. Above the table sits everything you would otherwise work out by hand, and every strike carries its own buildup tag and a sparkline of today’s premium.

  • 01
    The numbers that matter, in one stripATM IV, put-minus-call skew, PCR by OI and by volume, max pain, expected move, chain bias, lot size and days to expiry. In the clip: ATM IV 12.66, PCR 1.30, max pain 25250, expected move ±282, chain bias ▲ 48, 5 days to expiry.
  • 02
    Four mini chartsNIFTY today, open interest by strike, OI change today and the IV smile. Resistance and support strikes are named under the OI chart, so the walls are read for you.
  • 03
    Buildup on every rowSB short buildup, SC short covering, LU long unwinding, LB long buildup, next to OI change. Day range bars show where the premium sits today, with the open marked.
  • 04
    Key levels you can clickOpening range 25,116 – 25,256, resistance 26000, support 25000 and max pain 25250 are pinned above the table. Click one to jump to that strike.
  • 05
    Record any contract’s bookClick ◉ beside a call or put and it joins the heat recorder beside NIFTY, every second. In the clip 25000 CE gets its own 200-level book and 25000 PE a 20-level one, once the 200-level slots are taken.
  • 06
    One click from strike to chartClick a call or put and it opens on its own chart with order flow. The clip ends on 25350 CE: hover a candle and it reads Delta +194 lots (+47.3%), buy 302, sell 108.
How it helps you

You stop flipping between a broker chain, a PCR site and a max-pain calculator. Where writers are sitting, which side is adding and which is covering, and how far the market expects to move today are all on one screen, next to the chart you trade from.

SHOT 08 Smart money concepts

Structure, liquidity and the trade, drawn on the candles.

Smart money traders mark the same things on every chart by hand: breaks of structure, order blocks, gaps, and where the stops are. EIMC draws all of it as the candles form, and labels each mark with what it measured.

  • 01
    Structure, labelledBOS and CHoCH lines where structure breaks or turns, and every swing marked HH, HL, LH, LL. The trend reads off the chart without a single line drawn by you.
  • 02
    Order blocks and gaps that report their stateSupply and demand blocks show whether they are fresh or tested. Fair value gaps show how much is filled: FVG · 83% filled, FVG · 19% filled.
  • 03
    Where the stops are, and how hard they get huntedStop pools on the right edge with a ★ score and the share of past raids: STOPS ▲ 25,476.95 · ★38 · 33%. Sweeps are marked, and each stop hunt says how far it ran: STOP HUNT ↑2.3 ATR.
  • 04
    A trade with real numbersSHORT ENTRY 25,416.37–25,418.23, stop 25,421.77, first target 25,383.43 at 7.6R and a second at 17.8R. Resistance zones say how many times they held: 3× · fresh.
How it helps you

The hour you spend marking up a chart before the open is done before you look at it. More importantly, you can see where everyone else’s stop is sitting, so yours goes somewhere the hunt is unlikely to reach.

SHOT 09 Indicators & overlays

Every overlay in one dialog, and every colour is yours.

Overlays are grouped the way you think about them, each group shows how many are on, and every line says in plain words what it draws. Nothing is hidden three menus deep.

  • 01
    Grouped, with a countChart, Trend 1/8, Intraday, Smart money 9/10, Golden profile, Levels, DQRS setups, Classic levels, Volume and Oscillators. You see at a glance what is switched on.
  • 02
    Each switch explains itselfFair value gaps: open 3-candle imbalances with 50% line and fill %. Stop-loss liquidity: where retail stop-losses rest past highs / lows. You know what you are turning on before you turn it on.
  • 03
    Colours down to the hexEvery overlay has its own picker with a full palette, a custom colour, a hex field (#f23645 here) and an opacity slider. Premium and discount get a colour each.
  • 04
    RememberedEvery switch, colour and setting is saved, so the chart you close tonight is the chart you open tomorrow.
How it helps you

A busy chart is only useful if you can read it. Turn on exactly what your strategy uses, colour it so it stands apart from the candles, and switch the rest off in a click.

SHOT 10 Bar replay

Pick a candle. The future disappears.

Choose any point in history and the chart rewinds to it. Everything after that candle is hidden until you play it forward, candle by candle, with every overlay rebuilding as if it were live.

  • 01
    Click where it startsClick a candle, or pick Random or a Date. The marker reads Replay from Tue 17 Nov ’26 14:32: this candle and everything after it stay hidden until played.
  • 02
    Every overlay goes back in time tooStructure, order blocks, stop pools and the EMA 200 (25,427.53 here) are rebuilt from the candles before the clock, so nothing on screen knows what happens next.
  • 03
    Proven not to peekA test corrupts every candle after the replay clock and checks the payload stays byte-identical. If anything read the future, the test would fail.
  • 04
    Your speed1× to 1800× or Max, each candle formed tick by tick from lower-timeframe data.
How it helps you

You get a hundred practice sessions in a weekend instead of one a day, on real NIFTY history, without hindsight. Test a setup on days you have never seen, and find out whether you would really have taken it.
