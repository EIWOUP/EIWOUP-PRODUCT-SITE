04 Before you claim it

What to know.

Do I need a card for the three-day Pro trial?

No. The trial is granted, not purchased. It unlocks every Pro capability on live data for three days, and when it ends you drop back to the free tier rather than being locked out — the whole interface keeps working on demo (synthetic) data with no expiry.

How do the three perks stack?

They are independent. The trial costs nothing and buys you three days to judge the product. If you then take a yearly plan you pay for ten months instead of twelve. The founding lock holds that price for a full year. Taken together on Pro yearly, year one lands around 83% below twelve months of list-price monthly billing.

What counts as a founding licence?

The first 500 paid activations, across Basic, Pro and Elite, monthly or yearly. The counter is on total licences, not per plan, so it can run out on any tier at any time.

Do I need a Dhan account?

For any paid plan, yes — EIMC is a terminal for your own Dhan account, not a data vendor. You need the Data APIs subscription for chart data, and the Trading APIs as well if you want Elite's live orders. Access tokens expire every 24 hours, so you paste a fresh one daily. The free tier needs no credentials at all.

Can I change plan during the lock?

Yes. The tiers are additive — Pro is Basic plus the analysis engine, Elite is Pro plus recorded liquidity and live orders — so moving up adds entitlements to your token and takes effect on the next launch. Your founding discount carries across.

What happens if my internet drops?

Your licence is valid for seven days and refreshes on every launch that can reach the server. If the network is down, EIMC keeps running on the cached licence until it genuinely expires. One failed DNS lookup must never lock a paying customer out of their charts during market hours.

Is this investment advice?

No. EIMC is an analysis tool. It measures what has happened and shows what is resting in the book; the DQRS hit rates it reports are measured on the symbol and timeframe in front of you and are evidence, not forecasts. Every trading decision is yours.
