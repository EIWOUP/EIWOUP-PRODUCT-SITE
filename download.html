01 Install

What the installer does.

Runs without administrator rights

A per-user install on purpose. No UAC prompt — and, the reason that matters later, an auto-updater can replace the files without elevation.

Installs WebView2 only if it is missing

EIMC runs in a native Microsoft Edge WebView2 window. The installer carries the evergreen bootstrapper and runs it silently when the runtime is not already present.

Creates shortcuts, offers a desktop icon

Start-menu entry always; the desktop shortcut is a tick-box during setup.

Never writes inside the install folder

Your charts, drawings, recordings and sign-in live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EIMC and are left alone by both install and uninstall. Override with EIMC_DATA_DIR.

A SmartScreen warning is possible. Until the code-signing certificate is in place, Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” for a new publisher. Choose More info → Run anyway. Both the app and the installer get signed once the certificate is in hand.
Uninstalling. Settings → Apps → EIMC Terminal, or the Start-menu uninstaller. It removes the program and the WebView2 cache beside it, and leaves %LOCALAPPDATA%\EIMC untouched — so reinstalling later brings your layout, drawings and recordings back with it.
Check the download. The SHA-256 above is the installer's fingerprint. In PowerShell, Get-FileHash .\EIMC-Setup-*.exe must print the same value; if it does not, do not run the file and tell us.
02 Sign in

Sign in once. That is the whole setup.

No broker account, no API subscription, no token to paste each morning. EIMC brings the live market to the terminal itself.

Make an EIMC account

Sign up with your email and mobile number. Every new account gets a 1-day Pro trial, no card needed.

Open the terminal and sign in

Use your client ID or email and your password. The terminal stays signed in on that PC, so you do this once.

The market is live

On Pro (or the trial), NSE, BSE and MCX data streams in by itself. On Free, every mode runs on demo data.

Your plan follows you. Subscribe, renew or cancel on this website; the terminal picks the change up within the hour, or at once when you restart it.
PCs. Your plan works on a set number of PCs at a time. Moving to a new PC? Remove the old one under Account → Licence & devices, then sign in on the new one.
We never ask for a broker login. EIMC will never ask for a Dhan or any other broker password, OTP or access token. Anyone who does is not us.
