Why EIMC

Every claim on this site, and how to check it.

Trading software is sold on assertions, which is why nobody believes any of it. This page is the opposite document: what the alternatives actually cost, why none of them covers NIFTY 50, the test file behind each number we quote, and a list of the things EIMC deliberately will not promise you.

01 The thesis

An index is the hardest thing to see into, and the most traded.

NIFTY 50 is a calculated number. It does not trade, so it has no order book, no volume and no tape. Everything an order-flow platform is built to read simply does not exist on it.

That is not a gap in the software market, it is a property of the instrument — which is why every serious depth tool declines to draw on an index rather than faking it. The honest consequence is that the instrument carrying the most intraday risk in India is the one its traders can see into least.

There is a way round it, and it is specific rather than clever: the near-month future does trade, it does have a 200-level book, and it tracks the index by a basis you can measure. Record that book and shift it by the basis and you have a liquidity picture for the index itself.

Dhan exposes the 200-level feed. Dhan does not store it — there is no order-book history API, anywhere. So the recording has to be yours, running on your machine, from before the open. That is the part of EIMC you could not reproduce with a script over your existing charts, and it is the reason the product exists.

200

Levels, not five

The deepest feed Dhan offers, with automatic fallback to 20 then 5 if a socket is taken. Bookmap’s Indian route topped out at five.

1s

From 09:00, app or no app

Fifteen minutes before the open, every market day, whether a browser is pointed at it or not. Nothing else records this.

5

Sessions you can scroll back through

A week of second-by-second book on disk. Tuesday’s wall is still there on Friday, with the tape that ate it.

04 The other list

What this will not do for you.

A page of advantages with no limits on it is an advertisement. These are the things EIMC is asked for and does not do, stated plainly so you can rule it out quickly if one of them matters.

NO SIGNALS

It will not tell you what to buy

There is no alert that says go long. EIMC measures what has happened and draws what is resting in the book. The DQRS grades and hit rates are evidence about a rule’s past behaviour on your symbol, not a forecast of the next trade. Every decision stays yours, and trading involves a risk of loss.

NO PROFIT CLAIM

It will not make you profitable

We have no idea what your results will be and will not pretend otherwise. The arithmetic on the pricing page is about the cost of the tool in points, not about returns. Nobody on this site will show you an equity curve.

NO BACKTESTER

It is not a strategy backtesting platform

There is no scripting language and no portfolio-level backtest engine. The forward test exists only to measure the five built-in DQRS rules. If you want to write and optimise your own strategies, this is the wrong tool and something like a dedicated backtesting product is the right one.

NO DATA FEED

It is not a data vendor

EIMC is a terminal for your Dhan account. You need Dhan with the Data APIs subscription, and for Elite’s live orders the Trading APIs too. Access tokens expire every 24 hours, so you paste a fresh one each day — that is Dhan’s design, not ours, and we will not claim it away.

WINDOWS ONLY

No macOS or Linux build today

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. It is a per-user install with no administrator rights and no UAC prompt, so it runs on a locked-down laptop — but if you are on a Mac, today the answer is no.

NO EQUITIES DEPTH

Index depth is a reconstruction, and says so

The NIFTY 50 heatmap is the near-month future’s book shifted by the basis. That is the honest description of it: it is the future’s liquidity, mapped onto the index’s price axis, because the index has none of its own. Anyone claiming genuine index depth is selling you the same reconstruction without mentioning it.

05 Fit

Worth it, and not worth it.

This is built for you if

  • You trade NIFTY 50, BANKNIFTY or SENSEX intraday, where the index is the instrument and depth has never been available
  • You have been stopped out at the exact low often enough to suspect it is not coincidence
  • You already read structure — BOS, order blocks, sweeps — and want the liquidity underneath it rather than more lines on top
  • You trade options and want to know who is winning the chain, not just what the open interest is
  • You have a Dhan account with the Data APIs, and would rather not rent market data a second time
  • You want to practise — bar replay at up to 1800× on history that provably cannot cheat

Buy something else if

  • You invest positionally and look at a weekly chart twice a month — none of this will pay for itself
  • You want automated signals or a bot to follow
  • You need a scripting language and a portfolio backtester
  • You are on macOS or Linux
  • You trade with a broker other than Dhan and do not want to open one
  • You want US futures or crypto order flow — that is genuinely what ATAS and Bookmap are for, and they are better at it than we would be
